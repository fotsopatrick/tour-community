# Third-party modules bundled here

These folders are **not our work**. They are bundled because our own modules
depend on them, and they are redistributed under their own licences, with
their original authors and notices intact.

| module | author | licence |
|---|---|---|
| `web_responsive` | Odoo Community Association (OCA) and contributors | LGPL-3 |
| `web_dark_mode` | Odoo Community Association (OCA) and contributors | LGPL-3 |

`tour_de_controle_theme` depends on both. Without them the tower loads but
renders unstyled.

Everything else under `custom-addons/` is ours.
